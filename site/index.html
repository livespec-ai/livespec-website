Two goals

livespec is an experimental work in progress. It explores two goals:

1. A circular feedback loop between specification and implementation

A living, natural-language specification drives the implementation. What building and running the implementation reveals (gaps, drift, surprises) flows back into the specification through a governed revision process, so the two never silently diverge.

2. Every quality dial turned as close to 11 as possible

Fast, cheap, right: the old triangle says pick two. livespec asks how far "right" can be pushed (testing, review, verification, documentation) while still staying inside real cost and speed constraints.
